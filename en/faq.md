## 1. About Workdrop

### What does Workdrop do?
Workdrop keeps images you take for work, such as receipts, whiteboards, site photos and reference screenshots, separate from your personal gallery. Images taken with the app's camera or shared into it from other apps are stored only inside the app and are deleted automatically when the keep time you set (24 hours by default) ends.

### Why don't my photos show up in the gallery? Where are the files stored?
They are stored in app-only storage (the device's internal storage) that only this app can access. That is why they do not appear in your gallery, in other apps or in cloud backups. If you want to keep an image in your gallery, export it (section 4).

### Does the app send my photos over the internet?
No. The app does not upload photos automatically. There are no developer servers, accounts or ads, and the app includes no analytics tools. Purchases are handled by the Google Play Store, and review requests go through the Google Play app. Copies you share or export yourself follow the policies of that app or storage.

## 2. Deletion

### When exactly are items deleted after the keep time ends?
When the keep time ends, the item immediately disappears from everywhere in the app. The file is deleted within 1 minute while the app is open, or at the next time Android allows while the app is closed. Battery saver, battery optimization and manufacturer settings can delay this, and deletion at an exact time is not guaranteed. If you force-stop the app, files are deleted the next time you open it. Until then, the file stays only in the app's internal storage and cannot be opened by other apps.

### How do I change the keep time?
You can choose 1 hour, 24 hours, 3 days, 7 days, custom (1 hour to 30 days) or "until a set time". Change it with the keep time chip on the camera screen, [Change keep time] on the import complete screen, or "Default keep time" in Settings.

### What happens if I change the device clock?
Deletion times are calculated from the device clock. Moving the clock forward deletes items earlier; moving it back deletes them later. Changing only the time zone does not affect the remaining time.

### Do I get a notification before items are deleted?
No. The app does not send notifications. When you open the app, items that will be deleted within 1 hour are shown above the list, and you can extend them all by 24 hours from there.

## 3. Keeping items longer

### How many times can I extend an item?
Each tap adds 24 hours and there is no limit on the number of taps, but no item is kept more than 30 days after it was saved. If you need it longer, export it to your gallery or a folder.

## 4. Sharing and exporting

### Are photos I sent also deleted after a day?
The item inside Workdrop disappears when its keep time ends, and the link you sent can no longer be opened again. But copies already saved by the receiving app, such as a messenger or email, follow that app's policy and Workdrop cannot delete them.

### If I delete an item now, are photos I already sent deleted too?
No. Copies you sent in a messenger or exported to the gallery or a folder stay in that app or storage.

### What happens when I export to the gallery?
A copy is saved in the `Pictures/Workdrop` folder and may be backed up by services such as Google Photos. Workdrop does not delete this copy. The item in the app still expires at its original time.

### Where does folder export save files?
A copy is saved in the folder you chose (a device folder or a document provider such as Drive) and follows that storage's policy. If the app is closed while saving, an incomplete file may be left behind, so please check.

## 5. Importing and taking photos

### How do I add screenshots? What files can I import, and how many?
Share the screenshot or photo from another app and choose Workdrop in the share list. You can import JPEG, PNG, WebP, HEIC and GIF files, up to 100 at a time and up to 50 MB each. Nothing is saved if the device has less than 200 MB of free space.

### Workdrop doesn't appear in the share list
If you select images together with videos or documents, the sharing app may send them as "all files", and Workdrop may not appear in the list. Please select only images and share them.

### Can I switch to another app while importing?
Yes, importing continues. If the device paused the app, it picks up where it left off when you come back. If you come back after more than 30 minutes, please share the remaining images again.

### I don't see the [Delete originals] button
The original can be deleted only when the sharing app tells Workdrop which item in the device gallery it is. If you share from Google Photos or some other apps, delete the original yourself in the gallery app.

### I deleted the original, but it's still in Google Photos
Deleting the original removes the photo on the device. Copies already backed up to the cloud must be deleted separately in that service.

### What happens to location information (EXIF) in imported images?
Images shared into the app are stored exactly as received, so capture and location information in the original may remain and is included again when you share or export them. The app does not add or change this information.

### Do photos I take contain location information?
The app does not request location permission and does not add location information to photos it takes. Images imported from other apps are stored as received, so any information they already contained may remain.

### Can I turn off the shutter sound?
The shutter sound follows the system settings of your device and region, and the app has no option to turn it off.

## 6. Widgets and quick launch

### The "N active images" count on the home widget doesn't go down right away
Widgets are redrawn only when Android decides. The count is recalculated when you add, delete or extend items, when you open the app, and when expiry processing runs. If the app is closed and the device is in battery saver, or you force-stopped the app, expired items may be counted for a while. The widget shows only the count, never images, and expired items disappear from the app right away.

### The widget turned gray after I force-stopped the app
From Android 15, the system disables widgets of apps that were force-stopped. Open the app once and the widget works again.

## 7. Backup and security

### Are my photos moved when I change devices or back up my phone?
No. They are excluded from both cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, and settings also start from defaults. Reinstall the app from the Play Store with the Google account you bought it with. Export any images you need to move to your gallery or a folder before switching devices.

### How are my photos protected?
They are stored in app-only storage that only this app can access, so they do not appear in other apps, the gallery or backups. They are protected by the Android device's own storage protection; the app does not apply extra protection of its own to the files. We recommend using a device screen lock.

### How do I use app lock? Does app lock make my photos safe?
When you turn on app lock in Settings, you need your fingerprint, face or the device screen lock (PIN, pattern or password) to see images in the app. Choose when it locks: immediately, after 1 minute or after 5 minutes. A device screen lock must be set up to turn it on. App lock hides your images when you hand over your phone for a moment; taking photos and importing still work while it is locked. It is not a security feature that protects against technical attacks such as on rooted devices.

### App lock turned itself off
If you remove the device screen lock, there is no way left to unlock the app, so app lock turns off automatically.

### On Android 12 or earlier, I can't take screenshots when "Hide in Recents" is on
On those versions, hiding the app in Recents and blocking screen capture work the same way. Turn it off for a moment when you need to capture.

## 8. Purchase and refunds

No. One purchase gives you every feature, including features added in later updates. There are no ads either.

### Do I have to buy it again if I change devices or reinstall?
No. Reinstall it from the Play Store with the Google account you bought it with. Items and settings are not backed up, so the app starts with defaults.

## 9. Language

### Can I use only the app in a different language?
Choose Korean, English or Japanese in Settings → Language. On Android 13 and later, you can also change it in system settings → App languages.

### The menu I get when long-pressing the app icon is in a different language
The home screen (launcher) may show the menu in the device language. The language inside the app stays the same.

## 10. Permissions

### I denied camera permission. How do I turn it back on?
Use [Allow camera] or [Open settings] on the notice card above the list. Importing by sharing works even without the permission.
