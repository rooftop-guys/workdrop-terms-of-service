RooftopGuys (루프탑가이즈, "the developer") makes and sells the Android app **Workdrop** ("the app"). This policy explains what information the app keeps, where it keeps it and when it is deleted, and what information the developer receives outside the app. The developer complies with the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea and other applicable laws.

## At a glance

- The app does not collect personal information and does not send it to the developer. There are no accounts, sign-in, ads, analytics tools or developer servers.
- Photos and screenshots are stored in app-only storage that only this app can access. They do not appear in your gallery, in other apps or in cloud backups.
- When the keep time (24 hours by default) ends, the item disappears from the app and its file is deleted. Uninstalling the app removes everything the app stored.
- Purchases and review requests are handled by Google Play.

## 1. Personal information collected or sent

The app **does not collect** your personal information and **does not automatically send** anything to the developer or to third parties.

- There is no sign-up, sign-in or account.
- There are no ads, no third-party analytics tools and no usage tracking of the developer's own.
- The app does not upload photos automatically, and the developer runs no servers.

The purposes, categories and retention periods of personal information processed by the developer inside the app therefore do not apply. Information the developer receives when you contact them directly is described in section 10.

## 2. Information stored on your device

To provide its features, the app stores the following **only on your device**. The developer cannot see it.

| Information | What it is | When it is deleted |
|---|---|---|
| Images | Photos taken with the app's camera and images shared into the app from other apps (such as screenshots) | When the keep time ends or you delete them |
| Thumbnails | Small images for the list | Together with the original |
| Item details | Values needed to manage items, such as save time, expiry time and file size | Together with the original |
| Settings | Default keep time, app lock, hiding the app in Recents, language, theme and similar settings | When you uninstall the app |
| Dates for review requests | The dates you used the share feature (last 30) and whether a review was requested. Used only to decide when to ask for a Google Play rating, and to show on screen how many of the last 14 days you used the app when you long-press "Version" in Settings | When you uninstall the app |

- Everything is kept in app-only storage (the device's internal storage) that only this app can access. It cannot be seen from your gallery or from other apps.
- It is excluded from Android cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer. If you change devices or reinstall the app, items and settings are not carried over and the app starts with default settings.
- **Retention and deletion**: You choose a keep time of 1 hour, 24 hours (default), 3 days, 7 days, custom (1 hour to 30 days) or "until a set time". Even with extensions, no item is kept more than 30 days after it was saved. When the keep time ends or you delete an item, it immediately disappears from everywhere in the app, and its stored file is removed by a later deletion task: within 1 minute while the app is open, or at the next time Android allows while the app is closed. Battery saver, battery optimization and manufacturer settings can delay this. If you force-stop the app, the files are deleted the next time you open it.
- **Uninstalling**: When you uninstall the app, Android removes everything in its app-only storage.

## 3. Permissions the app requests

| Permission | What it is used for | If you deny it |
|---|---|---|
| Camera | To take photos inside the app. Photos are stored only in app-only storage, and the app does not add location information to them | You cannot use the app's camera. Importing by sharing still works |
| Biometric authentication | To unlock the app with a fingerprint, face or the device screen lock (PIN, pattern or password) when app lock is on. Android handles the authentication; the app never receives or stores fingerprint or face data or your device password | Not used unless you turn on app lock |
| Run at startup | To set up the deletion schedule again after the device restarts. It runs only while there are items being kept | (Granted at install) |

The app does not request location, photos and videos (device storage), contacts or microphone permissions.

## 4. Information handled by Google

- **Purchase**: You buy the app on the Google Play Store before installing it. There is no payment feature inside the app, and neither the app nor the developer receives your payment details. Payments and refunds are processed through Google Play.
- **Review requests**: The app can show a rating window through the Google Play app. Google Play handles the window and any review you write; the app cannot tell whether you left a rating or what it says.
- **Error statistics**: If you have opted in on your device to automatically share usage and diagnostics data with Google, Google collects technical information such as app crashes, and the developer can see those statistics and error reports in Google Play Console. The app is built so that its error records do not include file paths, file names or image information.
- This processing follows the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en).

## 5. Copies you share or export

Copies that you yourself share to another app (a messenger, email and so on) or export to your gallery or a folder follow the policies of that app or storage.

- Copies you have already shared or exported are not deleted when the item in the app expires or is deleted.
- Copies exported to the gallery are saved in the `Pictures/Workdrop` folder and may be backed up by services such as Google Photos. Copies exported to a folder are saved in the storage you choose (a device folder or a document provider such as Drive).
- Links shared by the app can no longer be opened once the item's keep time ends.

## 6. Metadata of imported images

Images shared into the app from other apps are stored exactly as received. Capture and location information (EXIF) in the original image may remain and is included again when you share or export the image. The app does not add or change this information. Photos taken with the app's camera contain no location information.

## 8. Children

The app is a work tool for people aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. The developer does not collect children's personal information.

## 9. Your rights and how to exercise them

- Information in the app exists only on your device, so the developer cannot access, correct or delete it. You can delete items in the app yourself, or uninstall the app to remove everything.
- For the contact information the developer receives under section 10, you can ask at any time to access, correct, delete or stop processing it. Send your request to the contact in section 12 and it will be handled without delay.

## 10. Information received when you contact the developer

When you email the developer with a question or a refund or withdrawal request, the developer receives:

- Information received: your email address, your name (if you include it), the content of your message and any attachments, and (for refund or withdrawal requests) your Google Play order number
- Purpose: answering your question, processing refunds and withdrawals, and handling consumer complaints and disputes
- Retention: general inquiries are kept for 1 year from the day they are resolved, then deleted. However, the following records are kept for the periods set by Article 6 of the Enforcement Decree of the Korean Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce.
  - Records of consumer complaints or dispute handling: 3 years
  - Records of contracts or withdrawals, and of payment and supply of goods or services (to the extent the developer holds them): 5 years
- Deletion: when the retention period ends, the emails and attachments are deleted without delay (including from the trash).
- Where it is stored: support emails are stored in the email service the developer uses, Gmail (provided by Google LLC). See section 7 for details.

The developer can see the app's orders in Order management in Google Play Console. When you ask for a refund or withdrawal, the developer finds your order using the order number or email address you provide, and uses this information only to handle refunds and inquiries.

## 11. Safeguards

- Images and settings are kept in app-only storage that only this app can access, and are excluded from backup and device-to-device transfer.
- The app's records contain no file paths or file names.
- You can turn on app lock (biometrics or the device screen lock) and hide the app in Recents.
- Stored files are protected by the Android device's own storage protection; the app does not apply extra protection of its own to the files. We recommend using a device screen lock.
